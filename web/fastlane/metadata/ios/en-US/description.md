## Full Description

**Take back control of your finances.**

RustVault is a self-hosted personal finance app that runs entirely on your own server — your data never touches a third-party cloud. Import bank statements, track spending, set budgets, and gain insights with beautiful charts.

**Privacy-first design**
Your financial data stays on your hardware. RustVault connects only to your self-hosted server over HTTPS — no telemetry, no analytics, no ads.

**Import everything**
Supports CSV, MT940, OFX/QFX, CAMT.053, XLSX, QIF, and JSON. Works with Revolut, Monzo, Starling, HSBC, most EU banks, and US banks via OFX.

**Smart auto-categorization**
Define rules to automatically categorize transactions as they're imported. Rules match on description, payee, amount range, or account — no manual work.

**Multi-currency budgets**
Envelope-style budgeting with per-category limits and real-time tracking. Multi-currency with live exchange rates.

**Offline-capable**
View recent transactions and make changes offline. Everything syncs automatically when you reconnect.

**Biometric authentication**
Log in with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device fingerprint — your auth token is stored in the device's secure enclave.

**Import from your device**
Import bank statements directly from Files, email attachments, or any sharing source. No computer required.
