Take back control of your finances.

RustVault is a self-hosted personal finance app that runs entirely on your own server — your data never touches a third-party cloud. Import bank statements, track spending, set budgets, and gain insights with beautiful charts.

PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN
Your financial data stays on your hardware. RustVault connects only to your self-hosted server over HTTPS — no telemetry, no analytics, no ads.

IMPORT EVERYTHING
Supports CSV, MT940, OFX/QFX, CAMT.053, XLSX, QIF, and JSON. Works with Revolut, Monzo, Starling, and most EU and US banks.

SMART AUTO-CATEGORIZATION
Define rules to automatically categorize transactions as they're imported. Rules match on description, payee, amount, or account.

MULTI-CURRENCY BUDGETS
Envelope-style budgeting with per-category limits and live exchange rates.

OFFLINE-CAPABLE
View recent transactions offline. Everything syncs when you reconnect.

BIOMETRIC AUTHENTICATION
Log in with Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint — your token is stored in the secure enclave.
